<objective>
Replace the current 2-way `ThemeToggle` (which flips `resolvedTheme` and thereby pins
an explicit theme, losing "follow system") with a 3-way light/dark/system control
(D-02), and give the `dropdown-menu.tsx` primitive its first Gemba reskin (it currently
ships raw shadcn `bg-popover`/`border`/`shadow-md`/lucide tokens). Making "system"
reachable and re-selectable is a prerequisite for verifying the "system" column of the
DARK-02 completeness bar.

Purpose: Success criterion 3 requires every page/component to render correctly under
light, dark, AND system. The current toggle can never represent "system" as a distinct
state, so DARK-02 cannot be satisfied without this control.
Output: A reskinned `DropdownMenuContent`/`DropdownMenuItem` and a 3-way `ThemeToggle`.
</objective>

## Phase Goal (user story)

**As a** user on any page, **I want to** choose Light, Dark, or System from the header
theme control and have my choice persist, **so that** the app can follow my OS theme or
a pinned preference, and the whole app is verifiably correct in all three modes.
